# Market review — what else exists, and where we win

Researched 10 October 2026. Exchange rate used throughout: **₦1,330 / US$1**.
Prices are list prices for the cheapest paid tier unless stated.

---

## 1. Who we are actually competing against

Not Jobber. Not ServiceTitan.

A Nigerian solar technician today runs his business on **WhatsApp, a paper
docket, and his memory.** That is the competitor. Everything in section 2 is
priced in dollars and needs a dollar card, which puts it out of reach of the
person we are building for before he has even looked at a feature list.

This matters because it means we do not have to beat ServiceTitan. We have to
be **obviously better than a WhatsApp thread and a carbon-copy docket**, and
cheap enough that the decision is not a decision.

## 3. Is "offline" our edge? Partly — and we are not alone

I went looking for this specifically. The honest answer is that **offline is a
claimed feature almost everywhere and a real one almost nowhere** — but some
competitors have genuinely built it.

FieldPathPro's entire marketing page is an attack on fake offline:

> "Most 'offline' field service apps are fake… you can view scheduled jobs…
> you cannot submit reports, attach photos, or close a job. You wait."
> [3](https://fieldpathpro.com/offline-field-service-app)

They are right about the problem, and they are at ~₦51,900/month — **17× our
price.** Payaca, Zuper and Dynamics 365 all advertise offline capture with sync
[2](https://payaca.com/us/industries/solar-installers) [4](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/field-service/mobile/best-practices-limitations-offline-profile).

**Where we are still genuinely different:** every one of those is *cloud
software with an offline mode*. You need an account, you need to have synced
recently, and offline is a degraded state you fall back into. REHOTEQ is
**offline-native** — the whole app, every feature, indefinitely, with no
account at all. That is an architectural difference, not a feature flag.

Nobody else gives the full product away free forever with no signup.

## 4. Where we win

1. **Price** — 13× to 116× cheaper. Not a rounding error; a different category.
2. **Naira, no dollar card** — Flutterwave links. This alone disqualifies every
   competitor above for most Nigerian technicians.
3. **Offline-native, no account required** — the phone works in a basement, a
   village, or with no data bundle.
4. **Cheap Android, tiny bundle** — a PWA, not a 60 MB app.
5. **Proof of work** — signed job cards, GPS stamp, photo evidence,
   tamper-evident PDF, QR solar passport. **This is the sharpest thing we have
   and almost nobody is selling it.** Read section 5.

---

## 5. The wedge: we are not selling scheduling, we are selling proof

Every competitor above competes on *scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, routing*.
That is a crowded, expensive race against companies with hundreds of
engineers, and we would lose it.

None of them lead with this: **"the customer cannot argue with you."**

A signed, GPS-stamped, photo-evidenced, tamper-evident job card is a *dispute
weapon*. In a market where "you didn't do the work" and "it was working before
you came" are daily commercial risks, that is worth real money. It gets a
technician paid. It gets him paid faster.

We already have the whole thing built. We are not telling anyone.

**Recommendation: lead with proof, not with management.** The homepage line is
not "manage your jobs" — it is *"never argue about a job again."*

## 7. Going global

You said you want this globally acceptable. The honest sequencing:

The underlying need — *prove the work was done, with no signal, on a cheap
phone* — is universal across Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, India, Pakistan, rural
Latin America. Every one of those markets has the same "offline is a marketing
claim" problem. **The offline + proof combination travels.**

What does not travel yet: Naira-only pricing, Nigeria-only payment, and
English-only copy.

**Do not chase global now.** Global is a currency field and a second payment
provider, and it is worthless until Nigeria proves people will pay. Win one
market, then copy the model. Trying to be global on day one is how you end up
serving nobody.

## 8. Pricing

Your call — ₦3,000/mo, ₦30,000/yr, Business ₦15,000/mo — is **correct for
launch** and I would not change it. Adoption first; the price is not the
thing stopping you getting users right now.

Two notes for later:

- **Business is where the money is.** ₦15,000 for 5 technicians is ₦3,000 per
  head, which is the same as Pro. That is arguably underpriced — the supervisor
  dashboard and staff management are worth more per seat. Revisit once it
  exists and works.
- **Unit economics.** At ₦3,000/mo, after Flutterwave's cut and server costs,
  roughly 400 paying Pro users ≈ ₦1.2m/month. That is a volume business. It
  works, but it only works at volume — so ₦3,000 is a customer-acquisition
  price, not a forever price.

## 9. What the server unlocks, in order

The server is not a feature. It is the thing that makes four other things
possible at all.

1. **Accounts + entitlement** — real Pro/Business enforcement, real 14-day
   trial. Impossible without a server; fake without one.
2. **Sub-users / teams** — a Business account with technicians under it. This
   is the Business plan.
3. **Sync** — a job assigned on your phone appears on your technician's phone.
   This is what makes teams actually work.
4. **Supervisor dashboard (web)** — the lead engineer sees all jobs.

Order matters: 1 gates everything (you cannot charge until it exists), 2 is
the product you asked for, 3 is what makes 2 real, 4 can wait.

---

## 10. Recommendation

**Do not try to become Jobber.** We would lose, and the attempt would cost us
the two things that actually make us different — offline-native and Naira-cheap.

**Build the proof wedge, then go upmarket to teams.** Concretely:

- **Now:** company profile + site instructions (works today, no server).
- **Next:** the server — accounts, enforcement, 14-day trial.
- **Then:** sub-users and sync. That is the Business plan, and it is the
  first genuinely defensible revenue.
- **Later:** supervisor dashboard, then iOS.

And market the proof, not the management.

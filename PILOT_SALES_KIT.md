## 2. Face-to-face script (60 seconds, at the shop / on site)

1. *"Abeg make I show you something — 60 seconds."*
2. Open the app → demo job → **Generate PDF**. Hand him the phone.
3. *"See your name, your photos, location, signature. Customer signs here
   on your phone. After that, nobody can say you no do the work."*
4. *"E dey work without network. No account. E free for now while we test."*
5. *"Help me run two real jobs on am this week. Anything wey vex you,
   tell me — na that one I take build am better."*
6. Send him the install link on WhatsApp before you walk away. **If he
   doesn't install in front of you, he won't install at all.**

## 3. Pilot terms (say this plainly — free must still have rules)

- Free Pro for the whole pilot (target: 20 technicians × real jobs).
- In exchange: **report every problem** — what you tapped, what you
  expected, what happened, your phone model. Voice notes accepted.
- Your job data stays **on your phone**. Back up from Settings — if the
  phone dies without backup, the jobs die with it.
- After the pilot, Pro is ₦3,000/month. Nobody is forced to continue.

## 7. What NOT to promise (protects you from refunds and wahala)

- ❌ Never say "tamper-proof" or "cannot be faked". Say **signed, stamped,
  on record**. (The PDFs say this correctly since v1.0.5 — don't let the
  marketing say what the app refused to.)
- ❌ Never promise iOS, inventory, or in-app payments.
- ❌ Never promise AI diagnosis. The library is real answers from real
  jobs — *"not guesses"* — and that is the selling point until V3.
- ❌ Never collect money for Business seats until invite codes exist.

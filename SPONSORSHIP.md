## 1. What REHOTEQ Field is — in one sentence

**REHOTEQ Field** is an offline-first mobile workspace for Nigerian electrical, solar, CCTV and networking technicians that turns every completed job into signed, location-stamped PDF evidence in 60 seconds.

*Tagline:* **Diagnose it. Prove it. Get paid.**

---

## 2. The problem we solve in the real economy

Across Nigeria, competent technicians lose repeat business and get dragged into payment disputes not because their technical work failed, but because **nothing was documented**:

1. **No proof of work:** Customers dispute what was replaced, whether the earthing was tested, or what state the battery bank was left in.
2. **Unsafe guesswork on site:** Junior technicians guess at inverter and DB board faults instead of following an isolation-first check sequence.
3. **Cloud tools fail where the work happens:** Foreign field-service software assumes constant 4G, desktop dispatchers, and $40+/seat monthly pricing — none of which survives a basement inverter room or a rural site in Ondo or Edo State.

---

## 3. What is already built and running today

REHOTEQ Field is **not a pitch deck** — it is a working application deployed at **https://field.rehoteq.com**, verified by automated end-to-end checks on every release:

- **Offline-native by design:** Works with zero signal and no mandatory account. Installed directly to any Android phone from the browser.
- **Evidence-grade job cards:** Before / during / after / serial-number photos compressed to ≤350 KB for Nigerian data costs, with date, time, GPS coordinates, and technician name burned into the image pixels plus SHA-256 digests.
- **Dual sign-off that locks the record:** Customer and technician sign on the phone screen; once both sign, the job card locks read-only.
- **On-device PDF engine:** Generates branded Service Reports and Quotations (with proper vector-drawn ₦ figures and company logos) offline and shares them straight to WhatsApp.
- **Solar System Passports:** Generates a QR code per installation so the customer can check warranty status, serials, and maintenance history.
- **Grounded fault diagnosis:** Curated troubleshooting library from real Nigerian jobs, with mandatory isolation/safety blocks first and human-confirmed cause selection.
- **Business crews (offline & cloud-ready):** Company stamp across a 5-seat crew, invite codes, pinned site instructions, and supervisor tracking.

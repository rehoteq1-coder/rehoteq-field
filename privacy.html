Privacy Policy REHOTEQ Field

How the REHOTEQ Field app handles your data. Plain English, no legalese.

Effective 14 October 2026

Who we are

REHOTEQ Field is built and operated by Rehoteq Technologies, Okitipupa, Ondo State, Nigeria. Questions or data requests go to rehoteq@gmail.com or +234 703 630 2585.

The short version

Everything you put into the app (job cards, customers, checklists, signatures, quotations, photos) is stored on the phone you entered it on. If you use the app without signing in, that is where it stays. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

What is stored on your phone

  • Job cards, customer details, checklists, signatures and quotations
  • Photos (before, during, after and serial photos)
  • Your equipment register and solar passports, rebuilt from your job serials
  • Your profile: name, phone number, trade and plan

What is kept in the cloud when you sign in

Signing in with Google switches on sync and backup. When you do, these are stored on Firebase, which is infrastructure run by Google Cloud:

  • Your account email address, used as your identity in the app
  • Your profile: name, phone number, trade and subscription plan
  • Your job records, including customer details and photos, so they survive a lost phone and can be supervised from Field HQ
  • Feedback messages you send us from the app's Settings screen

We use these copies to run the service and nothing else. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to advertise to you.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Flutterwave, a licensed payment provider. We never see or store your card number. We receive only a payment reference and your plan status.

What the app never does

  • No advertising or ad tracking of any kind
  • No analytics or profiling SDKs
  • No reading of your contacts, messages, location or files beyond the photos you attach to a job
  • No sharing or selling of your data to third parties

Deleting your data

To delete your cloud data (account, profile and synced job records), email rehoteq@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will remove it. Local data on your phone is removed when you clear the app's site data or uninstall the app.

Children

REHOTEQ Field is a work tool for technicians and school administrators. It is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.

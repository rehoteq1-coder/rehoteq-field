“SMS inverter showing E03. What should I check?”
📚 matched from curated library · no AI call · works offline
⚠️ Safety first mandatory
Isolate the AC supply and switch the inverter OFF before opening any panel. DC strings remain live in daylight even when the inverter is off — cover the array before working on DC terminations. Do not work alone on a live system.
Possible issue 3 found
1
Loose or high-resistance DC connection
Voltage collapses under surge → overcurrent trip. Most common cause on systems older than 12 months.
HIGH
2
Startup surge exceeds inverter capability
Fridge compressor draws 4–6× running current for ~300 ms. Check inverter surge rating vs load.
MED
3
Battery / BMS current limitation
BMS cuts discharge on surge. Check SoH and BMS discharge limit against the surge current.
MED
Check in this order
1
Measure open-circuit battery voltage at the inverter terminals. Expect 50–54 V on a 48 V bank.
2
Repeat the measurement with the fridge starting. A drop below 46 V points to resistance or a weak bank.
3
Feel every DC lug for warmth after 10 minutes on load. Warm = resistance = the fault.
4
Re-torque all DC terminations to 12 Nm and re-test.
5
Read the inverter error log and confirm E03 clears under a controlled load test.
Parts typically needed
35 mm² copper lugs₦1,500 ea
Heat-shrink kit₦1,500
Torque wrench (if not carried)₦18,000
